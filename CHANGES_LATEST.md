## [1.4.1] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- **Center Remaining Casts mode**: You can now select **"Remaining casts (center only)"** as the center statistic in `/cm config` (or `/cm mode`), displaying remaining casts in the middle of action buttons (including utility/buff spells with a resource cost) while automatically disabling and greying out the bottom-right casts checkbox.
- **Remaining Casts Display Threshold**: Added a **"Display threshold (max casts)"** slider (`0–100`, where `0 = All`) in `/cm config` to only show the remaining casts counter when at or below your chosen limit — works in both center and bottom-right modes, including in combat with Retail *secret values*.
